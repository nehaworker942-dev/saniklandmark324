About Sanik Landmark

A real estate company built on trust, transparency and timeless design.

Our Story

Creating Landmarks in the Heart of Ajmer

Sanik Landmark Projects Pvt Ltd was founded with a simple belief: buying a property should be an exciting, secure and transparent experience. Based in Gegel, Ajmer, we develop and deal in residential plots, villas, apartments and commercial properties for families and investors.

We carefully select locations, ensure clear legal documentation and design projects that combine comfort, elegance and long-term value. As a RERA-registered company (Registration No. RAJ/P/2023/2737), we follow the standards of the Real Estate (Regulation and Development) Act, 2016.

  • RERA registered & compliant
  • Clear titles and transparent documentation
  • Customer-first approach at every step
